Risk · Tuesday · seven people and their bags in the 5008

This is no longer a blocker, because you are travelling with one small bag each. It is still the tightest thing in the plan, so it is worth checking before you go.

The Peugeot 5008 has seven seats. With the photographer you are seven people, so every seat is taken and there is no spare. On Tuesday you leave Bolzano and sleep in Brunico, so all the bags are in the car all day.

With all seven seats up, the boot holds about 166 litres (2017–2023 model) or about 348 litres (2024 model onwards). Six small cabin bags are roughly 120–150 litres, so they fit — comfortably in the newer car, and only just in the older one. Add the photographer's camera bag and the older car is full.

Monday and Thursday are easier. You are six people then, so one back seat folds down and you get about 300 litres. No problem at all with small bags.

Two things to hold everyone to. First, soft bags, not hard shells — a rigid case wastes the space a soft one gives back. Second, nobody brings a large suitcase: one big case alone uses most of the boot on the older model.

If somebody does end up needing more space, a roof bag on the roof rails solves it. But it makes the car about 2.0 m tall instead of 1.64 m, and Parking Walther only allows 2.10 m — so use Mondschein (2.40 m) or Castel Mareccio (no limit) that night. See C1 · 5008 model year, C2 · roof box.

B1 · Tuesday 14:00 · you cannot drive up to Alpe di Siusi

Your sheet says To Alpe di Siusi at 14:00. But the road up from St. Valentin to Compatsch is closed to private cars from 09:00 to 17:00. Cameras at five points check this. You cannot buy a ticket to pass. If you arrive in the afternoon, they turn you back.

You go up by cable car from Seis instead — free parking at the valley station, 15 minutes, €30 return each. At seven that is €210. There is a cheaper and better-lit alternative — see Tuesday, Open O2.

B2 · Tuesday 18:00 · the last cable car down is almost certainly 18:00, not 19:00

Your plan is to ride the cable car down at 18:00. The problem is that nobody knows whether 18:00 is the last car of the day or the second-to-last. The two official sources disagree.

  • If the website is right (last car 19:00) — you are fine. There is another car after yours. If you miss the 18:00, you catch the 19:00.
  • If the brochure is right (last car 18:00) — yours is the last car of the day. If it is full, or you arrive five minutes late, or one person is still in the toilet, there is no next one and seven people are stuck on the mountain.

The operator's printed 2026 brochure gives 07.09.–02.11.2026, 08:00–18:00 in both its German and Italian columns, and Tuesday 8 September is in that band. The English column has a typo; the website's 19:00 belongs to a band ending 6 September. Corroborated by the buses — Almbus line 11 and the Seiser Alm Express both withdraw their late runs after Sunday 6 September, so the whole network steps down on Monday 7 September.

Come down at 17:15–17:30 regardless. It costs 45 minutes on the plateau and removes the question entirely, and your 19:00 departure from Seis doesn't move. See C4 · the last cable car.

B3 · Wednesday 14:00 · Lago di Braies is gated at that exact hour

Your sheet arrives at Braies around 14:00. The 2026 access rule is Wed 1 July – Tue 15 September, 09:00–16:00, and 9 September is inside it. Arriving in that window without a pre-booked ticket means you are refused entry.

You can fix this for €25, and you do not have to change your free morning — see Wednesday, Open O3. But it must be booked ahead; it cannot be bought at the barrier.

B4 · Sunday · check-out and the flight do not fit together

Check-out is 10:00 and your sheet shows the flight in the 10:00–13:00 band. For an 11:00 departure you need to be airside by 09:00, so leaving Marghera around 07:15 — with luggage, on a Sunday, with no car, and well before check-out.

Two things fix it: the exact flight time (C10 · exact flight time) and a pre-booked van taxi the night before. Six people plus luggage is not a taxi, it's a minibus.

Book ahead because it's cheaper or because six people won't fit otherwise

4 · St Mark's Basilica — a timed slot at tickets.basilicasanmarco.it, €10. Tickets are named and non-transferable: six names spelled correctly, six photo IDs on the day, one slot. Walking up means queueing. Only if you decide to visit the historic centre — see Friday.

5 · Musei di Piazza San Marco — there is no Doge's-Palace-only ticket. The combined ticket (Palazzo Ducale + Correr + Archaeological + Marciana) is €35; the €30 rate needed booking 30+ days ahead and that window has passed. Secret Itineraries at €40 includes full admission to all four — €5 more for a guided 75-minute tour of the chambers and prisons, and booking is mandatory. Take the €40.

6 · Seceda, online. Not mandatory, but 5% off takes €74 to ~€70.50 and avoids putting a party of seven through a ticket window at 08:30 when the whole point is catching the first lift. Fix the headcount first (C6 · Seceda headcount).

7 · Every dinner, and Saturday lunch on Burano. A table for six is not a walk-in, especially in Venice on a Friday or Saturday in September. See §06.

8 · Two vans. Thursday VCE → Marghera, and Sunday Marghera → VCE. Six people with luggage is a minibus, not a taxi, and Sunday's is the one where being wrong means missing a flight.

No booking needed — don't let anyone sell you one

Seiser Alm Bahn (the Seis cable car) — turn up and ride. Scuola Grande di San Rocco — booking is not possible and not needed. Gallerie dell'Accademia, Peggy Guggenheim, LUMEN, MMM Corones, MMM Ripa — walk up. Braies boat hire — first boat 09:00, last 17:30. Venice transport passes — buy on arrival. And the Venice access fee does not exist on your dates. The 2026 contributo ran 3 April – 26 July only. Anyone selling you a September Venice access ticket is scamming you.

Mustergasse is named verbatim inside the Bolzano ZTL (Delibera n. 518 del 10.10.2022), which is active 24 hours a day, every day, with 14 automatic plate-reading gates. There is no open window.

Fine is €83–€332 per gate crossing. In and out is two fines, and they arrive up to a year later. See C3 · Fink and the ZTL plate.

Two things you do not need to worry about

The Venice access fee does not apply. The 2026 contributo di accesso ran 3 April – 26 July only, and the Comune closed the experiment on 27 July. For 11–13 September there is no fee, no QR, nothing to register. Anyone selling you a September Venice access ticket is scamming you.

Acqua alta is low-risk on your dates. ~75% of tides ≥110 cm fall October–December; September is the low-frequency shoulder, and MOSE has been operational since December 2020. Don't buy overshoes preemptively.

And no car in Venice. Piazzale Roma is €35 per non-divisible 24 h and buys zero extra metres — it's where the road ends, and line 6 drops you there anyway from 250 m outside your door for €1.50. Moot from Thursday 18:00, but worth knowing why the plan is shaped this way.

Eating: the short version

South Tyrol — order Speck Alto Adige, canederli/Knödel, Schlutzkrapfen, Gulaschsuppe; drink Lagrein or Vernatsch; finish with Kaiserschmarrn. Dinner starts 18:30 and kitchens can close by 21:00 — do not roll up in Bolzano at 21:30. Many places close Sunday and Monday.

Venice — the format is the bàcaro: stand at the counter, point at cicchetti, drink an ombra. In season now: sarde in saor, baccalà mantecato, bigoli in salsa, granseola. Not in season: castraure, frittelle; ask about moeche.

Two billing traps. Fish and steak are often priced "all'etto" — per 100 g. A €9/etto sea bass is €45 if it weighs 500 g, and this is the commonest way a Venice bill explodes; ask the weight and the total before ordering. And coperto is legal in Veneto, but under art. 18 of RD 635/1940 it must be disclosed before you order — if it isn't shown, it can't legitimately be charged. Tipping is not obligatory in Italy.

Walk five minutes off Piazza San Marco, the Riva degli Schiavoni or the streets around Rialto and the problem disappears. Photo menus and touts are the tell.
