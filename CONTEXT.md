# Context

Ubiquitous language for the Dolomites & Venice trip document. Glossary only — no
implementation detail, no plan content.

## The document

**Original plan** — the group's own Google Sheet: a seven-day grid, one column per
day, rows in hourly bands. It is the source of truth for *intent*. The document
never replaces it; it annotates it.

**Block** — one merged cell of the Original plan, e.g. `Lấy xe + Venice to
Dolomites + Lunch` on Monday 10:00–13:00. A Block is the atomic unit the
document annotates. Block labels are reproduced verbatim, in the group's own
wording and language, so a Block in the document is recognisably the same Block
in the sheet.

**Annotation** — a finding attached to exactly one Block. Every Annotation
carries exactly one Marker.

**Marker** — the severity of an Annotation. Exactly four, and they are not
interchangeable:

| Marker | Means | The reader's next move |
|---|---|---|
| **Blocker** | The Block cannot happen as written. A rule, a closure, a capacity or a clash forbids it. | Change the plan. |
| **Risk** | The Block happens, but a stated assumption may fail. | Decide whether to absorb it. |
| **Open** | Two or more defensible versions exist and the group has not chosen. | Discuss and choose. |
| **Fine** | Checked against a primary source, nothing to do. | Read and move on. |

A Blocker is never "expensive" or "crowded" — those are Risks. A Risk is never
"we haven't decided" — that is Open. *Open* is reserved for genuine group
choices, not for facts nobody has looked up yet; an unlooked-up fact is a
Confirm.

**Confirm** — an outstanding action with a named owner and a channel (phone,
email, or a person). Confirms are collected in one list and cross-referenced
from the Block that depends on them. A Confirm answers a question about the
world; an Open answers a question about what the group wants.

**Booking** — a purchase that must happen before a date or it becomes
impossible or more expensive. Distinct from a Confirm: a Confirm gathers a fact,
a Booking spends money against a deadline. Bookings are listed by deadline, not
by importance, because the deadline is the only thing that makes them urgent.

## The trip

**Traveller** — one of the six people on the whole trip. The six comprise **the
couple**, who are being photographed, and **the rest** — four people with no
obligation to be anywhere the shoot is. That distinction is what makes splitting
Tuesday possible; before it was written down, the plan implicitly assumed all
seven people did everything together.

**Photographer** — a seventh person, present on Tuesday only, who shoots and
does not otherwise travel with the group. He is a seventh *occupant* of the car
on Tuesday. Per-head costs are quoted **at six**, except Tuesday, quoted at
**seven**.

**Shoot group** / **light group** — Tuesday's two parties: the couple and the
Photographer, versus the rest. They travel separately and meet for lunch.

The shoot covers **both** Seceda and Alpe di Siusi (settled 17 Aug 2026). Since
the Photographer is present on Tuesday only, both mountains are fixed to
Tuesday and neither can be moved to another day. Tuesday's length is therefore
not negotiable; only its *headcount* is. This is why splitting the group is the
only remaining lever on Tuesday's pace.

### Named owners

Actions are owned by a person, not by "someone". Two roles are settled:

- **James** — the car: hire, driving, parking, tolls, ZTL plate registration,
  and the handover at VCE. Anything the vehicle touches is his.
- **May** — lifts and cable cars: Seceda, the Seiser Alm Bahn, and their
  headcounts.

Where a Confirm straddles both — the post-17:00 Compatsch ascent is a *parking*
reservation that buys *lift-avoidance* — it is owned by both, named explicitly.
Unassigned Confirms are written with `Owner: —` rather than being quietly
attached to whoever reads the document first.

**Drive phase** — Monday 10:00 to Thursday 18:00, the window in which the group
has the hire car. **Water phase** — Thursday 18:00 onward, Venice on foot and by
boat. Most constraints belong to one phase and are meaningless in the other.

**Base** — where the group sleeps: Bolzano (Mon), Brunico (Tue, Wed), Marghera
(Thu–Sat). A Base is not a destination; it is what makes a day's driving
distances what they are.

**Gondola** — ambiguous across this trip's two halves, so it is never used
unqualified. In Venice a **gondola** is the rowed boat, regulated by the Comune
at a price *per boat*. In the Dolomites, "gondola" is a common English word for
a **cable car** (Italian *cabinovia*) — Mont Sëuc, Seceda, the Seiser Alm Bahn.

The document writes **"Venetian gondola"** or **"the rowed boat"** for the first,
and **"cable car"** or **"lift"** for the second, never bare "gondola". This
matters beyond pedantry: one is priced per boat with a five-person cap, the
other per person, and the whole point of the money section is knowing which
charges are per head and which are per unit.

**Gate** — a physical or camera-enforced access control that refuses entry
rather than charging for it: the Bolzano ZTL, the Alpe di Siusi uphill ban, the
Lago di Braies permit window, the Tre Cime barrier. Distinct from a **Tariff**,
which merely costs money. Confusing the two is the trip's main failure mode —
arriving at a Gate with a wallet out does not work.
