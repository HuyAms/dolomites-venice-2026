## 2. Bolzano ZTL + parking — **the single highest-risk item**

### Mustergasse IS inside the ZTL, and the ZTL is 24/7

*Via della Mostra / Mustergasse* and *piazza della Mostra* are named verbatim in the official boundary resolution, **Delibera della Giunta Comunale n. 518 del 10.10.2022**, in force from 01/02/2023.
→ https://www.comune.bolzano.bz.it/Amministrazione/Documenti-e-dati/Documenti-tecnici-di-supporto/Delibere-della-Giunta-Comunale-n.-518-2022-e-n.-17-2023

Official visitor guidance, verbatim:
> "La ZTL del centro storico di Bolzano è attiva **24 ore su 24, tutti i giorni**. L'accesso è controllato da telecamere ai varchi che rilevano i numeri di targa… L'ingresso senza autorizzazione comporta una sanzione."
→ https://www.comune.bolzano.bz.it/Vivere-il-Comune/Guide/Accesso-ZTL-e-sosta-nel-centro-abitato

**There is no open window.** Anyone telling you "the ZTL is only active in the morning" is describing a different Italian city.

**14 ANPR camera gates** (official list → https://ztl.comune.bolzano.it/): via Museo–via Cassa di Risparmio · via L. da Vinci–via Cassa di Risparmio · via Kolping–via Carducci · via Alto Adige · viale Stazione–via Perathoner · via della Rena · via dei Grappoli–via Laurin · via Piave–via dei Grappoli · via dei Bottai–via Vintler · via dei Francescani–via Vanga · via della Roggia · piazza Montessori · piazza Anita Pichler · piazzetta Anne Frank.

### What a guest is actually allowed to do

Delibera 518/2022, verbatim:
> "agli **ospiti autorizzati degli esercizi ricettivi** … con ubicazione nella Zona a traffico limitato **è consentito accedere e sostare per il tempo strettamente necessario alle sole operazioni di carico e scarico dei bagagli**"

So: **luggage drop-off only, for the strictly necessary time, and only if pre-authorised.** It is not a parking permit — you cannot leave the car in the old town at all.

Official instruction to tourists (same guide page): *"Se il vostro albergo si trova nella ZTL, contattatelo in anticipo per comunicare la vostra targa e ottenere l'autorizzazione temporanea al passaggio."*

**Mechanism:** the *property* (Residence Fink) registers your plate with the Polizia Municipale. You cannot self-register — the online ZTL portal requires SPID/CIE/CNS (Italian digital ID) and is restricted to disabled badges, works, events, taxi/NCC.
→ https://www.comune.bolzano.bz.it/Servizi/Polizia-Municipale-Permessi-varchi-elettronici-ZTL-ed-aree-pedonali
- Polizia Municipale ZTL permits: **+39 0471 997799**, **A.3.P@comune.bolzano.it**
- Stated turnaround: max 20 days, average 2 days.

**The practical trap: you will not know your number plate until you collect the car at VCE on the morning of 7 Sep.** Arrange with Residence Fink *now* that they can register a plate at short notice, and send it by phone/WhatsApp from the airport car park before you drive off.

**Fine:** Art. 7 c.14 Codice della Strada — **€83 to €332** (→ https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:decreto.legislativo:1992-04-30;285). In practice the €83 minimum, 30% discount if paid within 5 days. **Each gate crossing is a separate offence** — in and out = two fines. For a rental car the comune requests your details from the hire company, and the 360-day service window starts from *that* handover, so a notice arriving 9–15 months later is normal.
→ https://www.europe-consommateurs.eu/en/topics/vehicles/fines/ztl-italy/

### Is the Monday plan feasible? — **Yes, but the constraint is the lift, not the drive**
Seceda's last operations on 7 Sep are around **17:30, not 19:00** (see §4). Working backwards: to be on the ridge in good light you want to be riding up by ~15:00, which means Ortisei by 14:30, which means leaving Bolzano by ~13:45. Landing 09:00 → airport exit ~10:00 → Bolzano ~13:00 → drop bags → garage → depart 13:45. It works with about 45 minutes of slack. If the flight is late, **skip the Bolzano stop and drive straight to Ortisei** (see the itinerary's Plan B).

**Sun position at Seceda, 7 Sep 2026 (NOAA, CEST):**

| Time | Altitude | Azimuth |
|---|---|---|
| 06:39 sunrise | 0° | ~82° |
| 15:00 | 43° | 219° |
| 16:00 | 36° | 235° |
| 17:00 | 26° | 249° |
| 18:00 | 16° | 261° |
| 19:43 sunset | 0° | ~278° |

**At sunrise the sun sits at azimuth ~82° — almost exactly where the Fermeda towers are.** You would be shooting straight into it: silhouette, dark rock faces. From ~15:00 the sun swings SW→W, i.e. **behind the photographer**, throwing warm frontal light onto the west faces of the Odle. The anti-solar point at 18:00 is azimuth 81°, precisely the Fermeda towers.

**Conclusion: the afternoon is the good light here.** The catch is that the lift stops ~17:30, so the best light you can shoot *and still ride down* is roughly **16:30–17:15** (sun 22–31°, warm and directional — already excellent). The truly golden 18:00–19:40 would require walking down. Given the stated hiking appetite, take 16:30–17:15.

## 5. Alpe di Siusi / Seiser Alm access — **the rules changed for 2026**

### The driving ban
The road from **Seis/Siusi (checkpoint at St. Valentin) up to Compatsch is closed to private uphill traffic 09:00–17:00.** Verified verbatim in the official 2026 mobility brochure:
> "Die Straße zum Landschaftsschutzgebiet Seiser Alm ist von 9:00 bis 17:00 Uhr gesperrt (Ausnahmeregeln für Gäste von Betrieben auf der Seiser Alm sowie autorisierte Fahrzeuge)"
→ https://www.seiseralm.it/media/f08d9eef-db7b-490c-9fbc-42ba1abcc812/20260514101734/sab-som2026.pdf

**Downhill travel is permitted at any hour.** → https://www.seiseralm.it/en/info-service/mobility/access-to-seiser-alm.html

**Legal basis:** Art. 8 "Verkehrsregelung" of the implementing rules of the supra-municipal landscape plan Seiser Alm, **D.LH. 259/V/81 of 10.02.1992** under Provincial Law 16/1970, cited on the municipal permit portal https://www.accesso-alpedisiusi.it/de. ⚠️ Full verbatim article text not retrievable online.

**When:** effectively year-round except the shoulder periods 15 Apr–15 May and 1 Nov–1 Dec. **8 September is squarely inside the restricted season.**

**Exemptions:** residents on the Alpe and their relatives; cableway/tourism/trade operators; owners and tenants of farms; suppliers; **guests staying in accommodation ON the Alpe** (free access on arrival day; during the stay, accommodation↔Compatsch before 10:00 and after 17:00); **European Disability Card holders** (any time, after registration); agricultural vehicles. Enforcement by **ANPR cameras at five points**: St. Valentin, Jochstraße, Compatsch, Pufels, Jender.
→ https://www.accesso-alpedisiusi.it/de (formerly zufahrt-seiseralm.it)

**A guest lodging in Bolzano has no way to drive up during 09:00–17:00.** None of the permit categories covers a day visitor staying off the Alpe. ⚠️ Whether a *commercial photoshoot permit* category exists is **UNVERIFIED** — the portal lists none. If the photographer needs mid-day vehicle access, ring the Comune di Castelrotto/Kastelruth and info@seiseralm.it / +39 0471 709600.

### 🚨 New for 2026: parking must be pre-booked and it is **single-entry**

### Gondola — the official regulated tariff
Read directly from the Comune's own page → https://www.comune.venezia.it/it/content/servizio-gondola (Delibera della Giunta Comunale n. 89 del 20/04/2023):
- **Daytime 09:00–19:00: €90.00 for a 30-minute tour**
- **Evening 19:00–04:00: €110.00 for a 35-minute tour** (note the evening ride is *longer* — most blogs get this wrong)
- **"Possono salire a bordo al massimo n. 5 persone."** The price is **per gondola, not per person** — for a couple that's €45 each.
- Longer rides are **pro rata by time**: 45 min daytime = €135, 60 min = €180.

**The common overcharges:**
1. Quoting **per person** — "€90 each" is a 100% overcharge.
2. Quoting ~€90 but delivering 20–25 minutes. **Note the start time.**
3. Applying the **€110 night rate before 19:00**. The switchover is 19:00 sharp.
4. **"Serenade"** — the singer and accordionist are **not in the regulated tariff** and are charged on top, often €40+ pp on a shared flotilla. Agree explicitly or decline.
5. Hotel/agency "packages" at €140–€180 for a standard 30-minute ride.
6. Claiming a surcharge for the Grand Canal or a particular route. **There is none.**

**Protect yourself:** book at an official *stazio* (the Comune lists them all with phone/email on that page — Danieli, Molo, Dogana, Trinità, Santa Maria del Giglio, San Tomà, San Beneto, Carbon, Santa Sofia, San Marcuola). State the Comune tariff, agree price and duration before boarding. Complaints: **041 041** or the DIME digital desk.

### Restaurant rules — coperto and price disclosure
- **Coperto is legal in Veneto.** There is no national ban; **Lazio is the only region that has outlawed it** (L.R. Lazio 21/2006).
- **But it is only chargeable if disclosed in advance.** The governing rule is **art. 18 of Regio Decreto n. 635 of 1940**, obliging operators to make prices publicly known — in practice: the list must be displayed outside, and every cost item must appear in the menu before you order. **If the coperto is not shown before you order, it cannot legitimately be charged.**
- Veneto additionally requires food and beverage businesses to display prices **with an indication of the service offered**, clearly legible from outside during opening hours, and to communicate any service-related price increase through the lists and menus. ⚠️ Regional index https://bur.regione.veneto.it/BurvServices/pubblica/DettaglioLegge.aspx?id=200297 — **exact law/article number not pinned; treat the Veneto citation as needing confirmation.** The RD 635/1940 basis and the Lazio-only ban are solid.
- Practical: coperto in Venice runs €2–€6 pp. **"Servizio" (10–15%) is separate** — some places levy both; both must be disclosed, and a restaurant charging service *and* expecting a tip is double-dipping (tipping is not obligatory in Italy). **Fish/steak priced "all'etto"** (per 100 g) is the classic bill shock — ask for the weight and total price *before* ordering. You are entitled to a *ricevuta fiscale*. Disputes: Polizia Locale **041 2747070** or **041 041**.

## 14. Mistakes to avoid

### Dolomites

1. **🚨 Trying to drive up to Alpe di Siusi in the afternoon.** Uphill private traffic is banned **09:00–17:00**, camera-enforced at St. Valentin. There is no afternoon ticket. Use the cable car from Seis.
2. **🚨 Assuming the last cable car down is 19:00.** The printed 2026 brochure says **18:00**. Until confirmed, plan for 18:00 — being stranded ends the day and the Brunico check-in.
3. **Arriving at Seceda for 08:00.** First ascent is **08:30**.
4. **Driving into Bolzano old town without the plate registered.** 24/7, 14 gates, **€83–€332 per crossing**. In and out is two fines, arriving up to a year later.
5. Assuming the Bolzano ZTL "switches off" at some point. It does not.
6. Booking a tall hire car and relying on Parking Walther's **2.10 m limit**.
7. Parking on white lines in the Bolzano centro zone — residents only.
8. **Planning Lago di Braies off 2025 information.** The 2026 window is **Wed 1 Jul – Tue 15 Sep, 09:00–16:00** — wider and earlier than 2025.
9. **Booking the Braies ticket same-day** — the €22 rebate inside the €44 ticket is only given in advance. And there is **no standalone €22 product**.
10. **Assuming the Braies lake loop is flat.** It is 3.6 km with 86 m of ascent and includes a stair path. Lago di Dobbiaco's 1.7 km / 20 min / 7 m circuit is the genuinely flat one.
11. **Detouring to the Tre Cime toll road from Misurina.** €40 per car, mandatory plate-based online booking, and the ticket dies the moment you exit. Val Fiscalina gives a comparable view for the price of a car park.
12. Assuming a guest card gets you lift discounts. Neither pass covers Val Gardena or Alpe di Siusi lifts, nor the Almbus.
13. Buying a Dolomiti Super Summer pass for Seceda — not accepted.
14. **Believing South Tyrolean huts are cash-only.** 19 of the 21 Alpe di Siusi huts that publish payment methods take credit cards, and refusing a card is illegal (€30 + 4%). Carry cash for dead terminals, not because you'll need it.
15. **Missing the hut kitchen window.** Hot food runs ~11:00/11:30 to 15:00/16:00. Be sitting down by 14:00.
16. **Forgetting hut rest days.** Your mountain days are Mon/Tue/Wed: Radauer and Untertrompedeller close Monday, **Gstatsch Schwaige Tuesday**, Rauchhütte Wednesday.
17. **MMM Ripa is closed Tuesdays** — the day you arrive in Brunico. Wednesday or never.
18. **Underestimating September at altitude.** ~9 °C max at 2,500 m, sub-zero dawns possible, ~40% of afternoon hours cloud-immersed. Layers and a wind shell.
19. Assuming shops are open on Sunday in South Tyrol, or museums on Monday.
20. **Letting Cortina overrun on Thursday.** 14:00 is a hard deadline; the car is due back at 18:00.
21. Returning the hire car without photographing fuel, mileage and all four corners — or without refuelling first.

### Venice

22. **Buying the €1.50 ticket for the airport.** It is not valid to or from VCE. The Aerobus ticket is **€10** and covers the onward interchange.
23. **Buying the €1.50 ticket and boarding a vaporetto.** It is land-only; the boat version is €9.50.
24. Not validating on every boarding, including with a multi-day pass. €64.50 minimum on the water.
25. **Buying a September Venice "access fee" ticket.** It does not apply on 11–13 Sep. Anyone selling one is scamming you.
26. **Paying a gondolier per person.** €90 per gondola for 30 min (09:00–19:00), up to 5 people. Serenades are extra and unregulated. Note your start time.
27. Sitting on bridge steps to eat — €100–200 plus a removal order. Feeding pigeons €25–500. Swimwear €250. Walking a bicycle €100.
28. Buying Basilica tickets from a reseller — named, non-transferable, and you can be turned away.
29. **Going to Torcello on line 12 mid-day.** It skips the stop entirely. Use **line 9 from Burano**.
30. Taking a "free launch to a Murano glass demonstration".
31. Going to the Lido on Sat 12 Sep — Film Festival closing day.
32. Ordering fish without asking the per-etto weight.
33. Spending both Friday and Saturday on the islands and never seeing St Mark's, the Doge's Palace, the Frari or San Rocco.
34. **Missing the Fri/Sat Doge's Palace late opening to 23:00.**
35. **🚨 Leaving Marghera at 10:00 on Sunday.** No car, Sunday service, and check-out clashes with the flight. Leave by ~06:40 and pre-book a taxi.

## 15. Mountain huts — verified from the 53-hut official dataset

Parsed from the Alpe di Siusi tourism board's hut database → https://www.seiseralm.it/de/aktiv/sommer/wandern/huetten-almen.html and spot-checked against individual hut sites.

### Hot-food windows — the real constraint
Of 43 huts publishing a "Warme Küche" window: **starts 11:00 (19 huts) or 11:30 (15)**; **ends 15:00, 15:30 or 16:00**. Examples: Baita Troier 11:30–15:30 (troier.com); Rifugio Firenze 11:30–15:00 with a reduced menu after (rifugiofirenze.com); Rifugio Bolzano 10:30–15:00 and 18:00–19:00 (schlernhaus.it).
Most huts stay open past that for drinks, cake and cold *Marende*; huts with beds run a second service ~18:00–19:30. **"Closed by 17:00" is true only of a minority — but the kitchen really does shut.**

### Payment — the "cash only" claim is false here
Of 53 huts, 21 publish payment methods: **Kreditkarte 19 · Bankomat/Maestro 17 · cash-only 1** (Saltnerhütte Tschapit). Even Rifugio Bolzano at 2,457 m takes cards.
Legally: D.L. 179/2012 art. 15 c.4 obliges card acceptance; **D.L. 152/2021 art. 19-ter** adds, from 1 Jan 2023, a penalty of **€30 + 4% of the transaction** for refusing a card *of any amount*. Only exemption is "oggettiva impossibilità tecnica". → https://www.normattiva.it/uri-res/N2Ls?urn:nir:stato:decreto.legge:2021-11-06;152~art19ter
**Carry €60–100/day anyway** for dead terminals — but a refusal is illegal.

### Booking, service, water
- **Lunch needs no booking.** Rifugio Firenze: *"Non si prendono prenotazioni per il pranzo."* Sanon says the same. Only 2 of 53 offer online table booking. Book only for gourmet huts, dinner, or overnight.
- **Table service, not self-service** — the board's own line: *"Große Selbstbedienungsrestaurants gibt's hier nämlich keine."*
- **Free tap water is not a norm.** The AVS Hüttenordnung prices tea water at €3/litre. Carry a bottle.
- **Flag flying = hut is staffed and serving.**

### Rest days — your days are Mon/Tue/Wed
Radauer, Untertrompedeller — **Monday**. **Gstatsch Schwaige — Tuesday.** Rauchhütte — **Wednesday.** (Dibaita Thursday; Ritsch Schwaige Friday; Edelweiss, Messner Schwaige, Wild-o-Williams Saturday.)

### Season
**Only one hut of 53 closes before 20 September 2026.** Most run to mid-October or into November. Your dates are peak-open — ~5 days before the first minor lift step-down (Col Raiser 13 Sep, Seiser Alm Bahn 14 Sep).

### Terminology
**Schwaige / Alm / malga** = working dairy hut; own cheese and bread, cheapest, closes earliest. **Rifugio / Schutzhütte** = beds and dinner, higher up. **Berggasthof** = licensed mountain inn, longest hours. **Baita** = tells you nothing; check the hours.

## 19. Food and drink — orientation

⚠️ **Scope warning.** The venue-level research pass for **Bolzano, Brunico, Val Gardena and Venice restaurants was not completed** — the search budget was exhausted. What follows is orientation, not a plan. **No named restaurant in those places has been verified.** The Braies venues (§16) and mountain huts (§15) *are* verified.

### South Tyrol — what to eat
The cuisine is Austrian-Italian hybrid. Worth seeking: **Speck Alto Adige PGI**; **canederli / Knödel** (bread dumplings, in broth or with salad); **Schlutzkrapfen / mezzelune** (spinach-and-ricotta half-moons); **Gulaschsuppe**; **Tirtlen** (fried filled pastry); **Kaiserschmarrn** (shredded sweet pancake with cranberry); **Apfelstrudel**; **Graukäse** (sour grey cheese, an acquired taste); **Schüttelbrot** (crisp rye flatbread); **Vinschgauer** rolls.
Wines are excellent and under-known: **Lagrein** (dark, local to Bolzano), **Schiava/Vernatsch** (light red), **Gewürztraminer** (from Tramin, south of Bolzano), **St. Magdalener/Santa Maddalena DOC** — grown on the hills immediately above Bolzano.
⚠️ **Törggelen**, the autumn chestnut-and-new-wine tradition, traditionally starts late September/October — **likely too early for 7–10 Sep. Unverified.**

### Venice — what to eat
**Cicchetti** — small plates at a *bàcaro*, eaten standing with an *ombra* (small glass of wine). The classics: **baccalà mantecato** (whipped salt cod), **sarde in saor** (sweet-sour sardines with onion and raisin), **folpetti** (baby octopus), **mozzarella in carrozza**.
Dishes: **bigoli in salsa**, **risi e bisi**, **fegato alla veneziana**, **granseola**. ⚠️ **Moeche** (soft-shell crab) are seasonal, spring and autumn — whether early September qualifies is **unverified**.
Drinks: the Venetian spritz is made with **Select**, not Aperol. **Prosecco** is local. A **Bellini** at Harry's Bar is famous and expensive.

### Practicalities
- **Meal times:** Italy eats lunch 12:30–14:30, dinner 19:30–22:00. **South Tyrol eats earlier** — dinner from 18:30, kitchens can close by 21:00. Don't arrive in Bolzano at 21:30.
- **Cards:** legally obliged; refusing one carries €30 + 4% (see §15). Mountain huts are **not** the exception people claim.
- **Coperto** is legal in Veneto (only Lazio has banned it) but **only chargeable if disclosed before you order** — the basis is art. 18 of Regio Decreto 635/1940. "Servizio" is separate; a restaurant charging both *and* expecting a tip is double-dipping. **Fish priced *all'etto*** (per 100 g) is the classic bill shock — ask the weight and total before ordering.
- **Booking:** anywhere good in Venice on a Friday or Saturday in September needs booking days ahead. Bàcari never do.

### Three consequences

1. **The post-17:00 drive up Alpe di Siusi now saves ~€150 as well as improving the light.** Parking is €30 per *car*; the lift is €30 per *person* — €180 for six. This makes the call to **+39 0471 709600** the highest-value five minutes of planning available.
2. **Tre Cime di Lavaredo becomes defensible.** At €40 per car ÷ 5 = €8 a head, and ~1 h from Brunico. The Rifugio Auronzo → Rifugio Lavaredo stretch on trail 101 is **completely flat, 20 minutes each way**. Workable Wednesday: Braies 08:00–10:45 → Misurina ~1 h → toll road → Rifugio Auronzo ~12:15 → flat walk and lunch → leave 14:30 → Brunico ~16:00, dropping San Candido. 🔒 Needs a pre-booked plate slot at pass.auronzo.info; the ticket dies on exit.
3. **The gondola fits exactly.** The regulated maximum is **5 passengers per boat**, so all five ride together for €90 — €18 each, against €45 each for a couple.

### Group logistics

- **Book every dinner.** A table for five or six is a different proposition, especially in Venice on a Friday or Saturday in September. The earlier note that bàcari need no booking holds for two people standing at a bar — **it does not work for five.** Split for the cicchetti crawl, or book a table.
- **Mountain huts take no lunch bookings** — with five, arrive before 12:30 or wait.
- **Basilica tickets are named and non-transferable** — five names, five photo IDs, one slot. Spell them correctly at booking.
- **Everyone validates their own ticket on every boarding.** Five people is five chances at a €64.50 fine on the water.
- **Sunday's airport taxi needs a van** for five plus luggage. Book the night before and state the headcount.
- **Check vehicle height against Parking Walther's 2.10 m limit.** Fall back to Mondschein (2.40 m) or Castel Mareccio (no limit).
